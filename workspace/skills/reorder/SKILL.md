# Reorder

People who name a product usually mean the one they always get. A fresh search finds the single unit at the lowest price, which is often not what they buy: a multipack, a bigger size, a particular brand. Their order history knows.

1. Look at their past orders first, on each shopping site your setup note says you're signed in to. Open these by URL and read them (no clicking needed): Amazon's Buy Again at `https://www.amazon.com/gp/buyagain` and an order search at `https://www.amazon.com/gp/your-account/order-history?search=<a word or two from the product name>`. Finish when you know whether they've bought it, the exact listing, its pack and count, what they paid, and when they last ordered it.
2. If it's ordered and not delivered yet, or arrived in the last few days, tell them before buying more. An order in their history is theirs unless this chat shows you placed it: say it's on its way, never that you ordered it.
3. If they've bought it before, their usual listing is the default. When what they said fits more than one listing (a size could be one unit or the multipack of that size they usually get, or the count is missing), ask in one short text which they want, naming their usual first with its current price. Don't answer with another store's single-unit price.
4. If they've never bought it, shop for it the usual way.
5. Placing the order follows your setup note and Acting on someone's behalf: the exact item, pack and total in front of them, and a clear yes.
